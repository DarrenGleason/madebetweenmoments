A LITTLE ABOUT ME

I believe the ordinary is often extraordinary.

I'm the photographer behind Made Between Moments. I love the honest connections, the small details, and the fleeting moments that make your story uniquely yours.

My approach is relaxed and connection-focused. You don't need to know what to do with your hands or how to make everyone look at the camera at once. Just come as you are, spend time with your people, and let the moments unfold.

This space is about remembering not only what life looked like, but how it felt to be there.
